Questions

What is Wake?

Wake is the gym for your unaided mind. It shows you how much of your thinking still belongs to you — your Unaided Ratio — then trains it: a daily 3-minute rep, a 20-second warm-up before the AI apps you flag, and a score that tracks the strength you build. No shame, no locks, no lectures.

When does it launch?

We're two founders building now, launching into the new iOS cycle this fall. Waitlist members get first access — and founding pricing as thanks for being early.

How much will it cost?

Planned: $79.99/yr — under the leading screen-time app ($99.99) with more inside: the mirror, the gym, the score. Founding waitlist members lock $29.99 for year one. A family plan (parents see patterns, never content) is planned at $129.99/yr.

What is the campus pilot?

A small cohort of instructors, integrity officers, and L&D teams testing user-initiated unaided verification this academic year. Students and employees earn a cheat-proof certificate during a verified no-AI window — and choose what to share. We never sell scores, and nobody is assessed without opting in.

Does it work for parents?

Yes — a parent view shows your teen's AI usage patterns (hours, kinds of use, late-night trends) without ever reading their conversations. Built for worried parents, not for surveillance.

What about AI used in a browser?

Phone coverage at launch, including AI websites used in mobile browsers, with a desktop companion in development.

Is AI use actually a problem?

Total time isn't. Dependency patterns are — and the research is catching up fast (APA, 2026; Brookings, 2026; Frontiers, 2026). The app measures the pattern, not just the clock — then trains it.
